### T-5080 · `hero-orientation` is wrong or absent on most real images
**Status:** Ready | **Profile:** P1-feature-worker
**Found by:** the JBComplete phenotype scoring, 2026-08-05. Supersedes the loose "CLIP orientation
argmax 74%" note carried in [[T-2600]] and [[T-4970]] with a measured, per-value breakdown.

`hero-orientation` is one of the CLIP-scored ImageNGP phenotype features. On real data it is either
wrong or absent for most images: one orientation value never fires at all, and the rest skew heavily
toward `UNKNOWN`/`BACK` over the correct value. This is currently the top blocker on phenotype
assignment accuracy.

**Problem(s):**
- Measured on JBComplete (100 images, classification on, shipped config), `hero-orientation`'s
  per-value output diverges sharply from ground truth across all six values:

  | `hero-orientation` value | times produced | times it should have been produced |
  |---|---|---|
  | `FRONT` | 18 | ~45 (25 `front-packshot` + 7 `front-on-model-partial` + 5 `front-on-model-full-product` + …) |
  | `BACK` | 36 | ~19 |
  | `UNKNOWN` | 37 | 0 |
  | `DIAGONAL` | 6 | 8 |
  | `SIDEON` | **0** | 9 (7 `side-packshot` + 2 `side-on-model`) |
  | `TOP` | 3 | 3 |

  Two separate failures underlie this table, itemized below.
- **Failure 1 — `SIDEON` is never produced at all.** The prompt exists (`ClipPrompts.json`: *"a side
  view of the product"*, value `SIDEON`) but never wins the argmax on any of the 100 images, including
  the 9 whose ground truth is a side view. Effect: `side-packshot` and `side-on-model` are unreachable
  on real data — recall 0/7 and 0/2. Consequence: two of the 18 phenotypes are dead for a reason no
  threshold move can fix, and `DetOrderRules` slot det2 ("side") can only ever be filled by overflow.
- **Failure 2 — `UNKNOWN` on 37% of images plus a front↔back bias on the rest.** Even after [[T-4970]]
  lowered the orientation bar to 0.33, more than a third of images still clear no orientation prompt at
  all, and `BACK` is produced roughly twice as often as it is true. Of the 25 ground-truth
  `front-packshot` images, only 4 read `FRONT`; 10 read `BACK` and 9 read `UNKNOWN`.
- This is the top blocker on phenotype accuracy, jointly with [[T-5070]]: on JBComplete the two
  together hold assignment precision at 9 correct out of 39 assigned.

**Test(s):**
- Live and failing in the test suite: `CiMiniGoldenTests.CiMini_Manifest_MatchesCommittedGolden` is red
  on family `94613033` for exactly Failure 2 above — all three Pareo images read `BACK` at
  0.346 / 0.400 / 0.476, so the packshot claims `bottomwear` det1 and the two shots the filenames call
  front (`_F1`, `_F2`) land at det5 and overflow. Ordering cannot fix this — see [[T-5060]], which fixed
  the compaction half of this family's ordering and deliberately left this orientation half red.
  (run: red, 2026-08-05)
- Dump the full per-prompt score vector for the 9 known ground-truth side-view images to determine
  whether `SIDEON` loses to a specific competing prompt or scores low in absolute terms — the two causes
  need different fixes (prompt rewording vs. a per-value bar). Do this first, before changing any
  wording. Not yet run.
