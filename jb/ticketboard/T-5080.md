### T-5080 · `hero-orientation` is wrong or absent on most real images
**Status:** Ready | **Profile:** P1-feature-worker
**Found by:** the JBComplete phenotype scoring, 2026-08-05. Supersedes the loose "CLIP orientation
argmax 74%" note carried in [[T-2600]] and [[T-4970]] with a measured, per-value breakdown.

**Measured on JBComplete (100 images, classification on, shipped config):**

| `hero-orientation` value | times produced | times it should have been produced |
|---|---|---|
| `FRONT` | 18 | ~45 (25 `front-packshot` + 7 `front-on-model-partial` + 5 `front-on-model-full-product` + …) |
| `BACK` | 36 | ~19 |
| `UNKNOWN` | 37 | 0 |
| `DIAGONAL` | 6 | 8 |
| `SIDEON` | **0** | 9 (7 `side-packshot` + 2 `side-on-model`) |
| `TOP` | 3 | 3 |

Two separate failures, not one.

**1. `SIDEON` is never produced at all.** The prompt exists (`ClipPrompts.json`: *"a side view of the
product"*, value `SIDEON`) but never wins the argmax on any of the 100 images, including the 9 whose
ground truth is a side view. **Effect:** `side-packshot` and `side-on-model` are unreachable on real
data — recall 0/7 and 0/2. **Consequence:** two of the 18 phenotypes are dead for a reason no
threshold move can fix, and `DetOrderRules` slot det2 ("side") can only ever be filled by overflow.

**2. `UNKNOWN` on 37% and a front↔back bias on the rest.** Even after [[T-4970]] lowered the bar to
0.33, more than a third of images clear no orientation prompt, and `BACK` is produced roughly twice
as often as it is true. Of 25 ground-truth `front-packshot` images only 4 read `FRONT`; 10 read
`BACK` and 9 read `UNKNOWN`.

**This is the top blocker on phenotype accuracy**, jointly with [[T-5070]]. On JBComplete the two
together hold assignment precision at 9 correct out of 39 assigned.

**Live in the test suite.** `CiMiniGoldenTests.CiMini_Manifest_MatchesCommittedGolden` is red on
family `94613033` for exactly this: all three Pareo images read `BACK` at 0.346 / 0.400 / 0.476, so
the packshot claims `bottomwear` det1 and the two shots the filenames call front (`_F1`, `_F2`) land
at det5 and overflow. Ordering cannot fix it — see [[T-5060]], which fixed the compaction half and
left this half red on purpose.

**Do this first:** confirm whether `SIDEON` loses to a specific competing prompt or scores low in
absolute terms — those need different fixes (prompt rewording vs. a per-value bar). Dump the full
per-prompt score vector for the 9 known side views before changing any wording.
