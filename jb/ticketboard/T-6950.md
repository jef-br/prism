### T-6950 · Direction 2: stop decoding at full resolution for scale-invariant analyzers
**Status:** Ready | **Profile:** P1-feature-worker
**Found by:** [[T-6910]], 2026-08-11 — deliberately not done in that ticket; split out so the decision
and the re-bless work stay tracked rather than sitting undecided in the archive.

The feature analyzers measure composition, geometry, colour distribution and exposure — all
scale-invariant. Decoding an 18.8 MP garment shot at full resolution to measure
`product-coverage-ratio` is waste; ImageSharp's JPEG decoder supports scaled decode.

**Why this is its own ticket, not a bolt-on to T-6910's parallelization fix.** Scaling the decode
changes the actual measured pixel values feeding the analyzers, which changes phenotype outputs on some
images. It cannot be done silently — the CiMini golden must be deliberately re-captured and re-blessed
against the new values, with a human confirming the new phenotype assignments are still correct, not
just different.

**The tradeoff (from T-6910's review, restated for whoever picks this up):** further wall-clock savings
on top of T-6910's parallelization, at the cost of a one-time re-bless-and-validate pass on the golden.
Whether that trade is worth it now is a product call — not decided here.

**Acceptance:** if taken on — pick a scaled-decode target size per analyzer (or a shared one), measure
the wall-clock win on SMASHEDLEMON45-scale data, re-capture and human-review the CiMini golden's new
phenotype assignments before re-blessing (not just re-run and accept whatever comes out), and record the
decision and its reasoning in `jb/docs/PRISM-match.md` or the relevant analyzer doc either way.

**Files:** `jb/src/core/Services/Matching/Classify/ImageFeatureAnalyzer.cs`, the `Analyzer_*` chain
consumers of decoded image data, `test/datasets/CiMini/expected-phenotype.json` (golden, needs re-bless).
