### T-5200 · SiblingPropagator loose-relation pass is unindexed O(unmatched × matched)
**Status:** Ready | **Profile:** P1-feature-worker
**Found by:** BE-tests matching-only run, 2026-08-10 — `X SMASHEDLEMON45` (1774 images) timed out at
45 minutes (JobID `3ddf1ad1-56d8-44b2-a36e-aaeddc9ab7d3`, `TIMEOUT_OR_NO_RESULT`). See
`matching-testlogs.txt` "ROUND 2" entries.

**The problem.** `SiblingPropagator.Run` (`jb/src/core/Services/Matching/Match/SiblingPropagator.cs:62`)
calls `FindLooseRelation` (line 146) once per unmatched image; `FindLooseRelation` does a full linear
scan over every matched sibling's rare-token profile with no index. That is O(unmatched × matched)
per job, each comparison doing `HashSet` subset/intersection work — no early exit, no bucketing by
token. At thousands of images this can blow up; the exact SMASHEDLEMON45 job never returned in 45
minutes even in match-only mode (classification skipped, so CLIP/Bracket 4/5 were never in play).

**Not yet confirmed as SMASHEDLEMON45's actual root cause.** Filenames and RefCo values line up
cleanly (`26000_775-725_*.jpg` against RefCo `26000-775/725` etc.) — Bracket 1/2 numeric concatenation
should resolve most of these images before they ever reach sibling propagation, which argues against a
large `unmatched` set. Before any fix lands: run the evidence harness
(`.claude/skills/prism-evidence-report/harness/EvidenceDumpHarness.cs`) against
`test/datasets/BE tests/X SMASHEDLEMON45` with `PRISM_EVIDENCE_SECTIONS=import,match` and confirm via
timing/instrumentation that SiblingPropagator is actually the hot stage before touching its algorithm.

**Fix direction once confirmed:** index matched profiles by token (inverted index: token → matched
records carrying it) so a loose-relation lookup for one unmatched image only scans matched records that
share at least one rare token, not the full matched set.

**Explicit scope boundary:** this ticket is about the algorithm, not about capping or timing out the
job — a configurable pipeline-level job timeout is being added separately (see `Prism_Config.json`) as
a guard rail regardless of whether this fix lands. Do not conflate the two: a timeout prevents a hang
from blocking the queue forever, it does not make the O(n²) pass fast.
