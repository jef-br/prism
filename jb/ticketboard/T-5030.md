### T-5030 · Normalize every input to JPG on white
**Status:** Ready | **Profile:** P1-feature-worker

Drop the separate handling path for images with an alpha channel. Every input becomes a JPG composited
onto a white background at import, before any analysis runs.

**Why now.** The trade-off is deliberate: getting the extra signal an alpha channel carries is worth
less than having every downstream stage look at one homogeneous kind of data. Two paths through import
means every analyzer, every threshold and every measurement has to be qualified with "…unless the
image had alpha", and none of them currently are.

**What the alpha in the repo actually looks like.** Measured on the 13 PNGs in
`test/datasets/JBComplete/` (2026-08-04): the alpha is a **feathered glow**, not a hard clipping path.
Alpha ramps 0→255 over a wide band around the product rather than switching at the product edge. So
the signal is not even the clean one the separate path assumes:

- Anything reading `clipping-path = true` from "this file has an alpha channel" is reading a soft glow.
- Anything deriving a subject box from `alpha > 0` ([[T-4960]]) gets a box noticeably larger than the
  product.

That is a second, independent reason to stop treating alpha as trustworthy evidence.

**What to do:**
- [ ] Composite alpha onto white during import normalization; emit JPG.
- [ ] Remove `AlphaSubjectCapture` (`jb/src/core/lib/Ingress/AlphaSubjectCapture.cs`) and every
      branch that keys off an alpha channel being present.
- [ ] Decide what `clipping-path` means once alpha is gone — it is a required feature on all six
      `ghost-*` phenotype rules in `ImageRoles.json`. Either it becomes a measured property of the
      white background, or those rules lose the `clipping-path` alternative. **This is a product
      decision, not an implementation detail — do not pick one silently.**
- [ ] Re-check [[T-4960]] (alpha box vs colour fallback) and [[T-4950]] (SubjectMask on the wire),
      both of which may be wholly or partly obsoleted by this change.
- [ ] Confirm the 13 JBComplete PNGs and the FILA94 transparent set still import and match.

**Files:** `jb/src/core/lib/Ingress/Importer.cs`, `jb/src/core/lib/Ingress/AlphaSubjectCapture.cs`,
`jb/src/core/Services/Matching/Analyzers/`, `jb/src/core/config/ImageRoles.json`.
