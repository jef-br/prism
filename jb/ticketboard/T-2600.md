     | | coverage | misassignment (of all rows) | precision (of assigned) |
     |---|---|---|---|

     The M11 bar is <5% misassignment. JBComplete is at 30%. The two numbers do not conflict: SPACINI29
     is homogeneous on-model apparel where only two phenotypes ever fire and they fire correctly;
     JBComplete has bags, footwear, ghost shots and packshots, and there the rules collapse. **The
     5% figure was never a pass — it was the artefact of a dataset that only exercises 2 of 18
     phenotypes.** Worst per-label recall: `front-packshot` **0/25**, `diagonal-packshot` 0/8,
     `side-packshot` 0/7, `front-on-model-full-product` 0/5. Worst per-assignment precision:
     `closeup-image` 0/8, `front-packshot` 0/3, `back-packshot` 1/7.
   - **Two causes account for nearly all of it**, both already ticketed: [[T-5070]] (the
     `intersection-count = 0` gate makes 75% of images unable to reach any packshot phenotype, and
     `closeup-image` sinks what it rejects) and [[T-5080]] (`hero-orientation` reads `UNKNOWN` on 37%
     and never once produces `SIDEON`). Neither is a threshold; both must land before step 4 is
     re-measured.
   - The 16 low-confidence rows need the human pass README §4.3 asks for, but they are **not** what
     is holding the number down — excluding them moves misassignment by 1.4 points.
