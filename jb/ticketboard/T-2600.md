### T-2600 · M5 Classify groundwork
**Status:** Blocked | **Profile:** P0-orchestrator

Tracks the 2 items in `jb/src/core/Services/Matching/Classify/jbtodo.md`: taxonomy reconciliation
(item 1, settled) and phenotype production validation (item 2, the only open item). M5 gate needs
item 1 (done) plus the ONNX shared session (done, 2026-06-29) plus item 2 closing after step 1 of its
chain below; the remaining steps continue afterward toward the full M11 bar of <5% misassignment
across all 18 phenotypes.

**Problem(s):**
- **Item 1 — taxonomy reconciliation: settled.** [[T-4700]] trimmed to real/reachable features;
  [[T-4800]] re-declared `shadow-present`; [[T-4970]] dropped `occlusion-level` and added
  `back-on-model-partial`; [[T-5040]] then cut the three `ghost-*` entries. Stands at **18 phenotypes**
  (verified against `ImageRoles.json` on 2026-08-05 — this ticket said 21 until then), every feature
  with a live producer. Process documented in `jb/docs/ImageNGP/HowToAddAPhenotype.md`. Status: done —
  tick this box when item 2 closes.
- **Ordering constraint (permanent, architectural).** `SubjectDetector` runs in
  `ImageFeatureAnalyzer.Refine` wave 3, *before* `FinalizePhenotype`
  (`jb/src/core/Services/Matching/Classify/ImageFeatureAnalyzer.cs:152-157`). Any detector-backed
  feature written after the rules evaluate reads UNKNOWN forever, making every phenotype requiring it
  unreachable. Not an action item — a standing fact any future phenotype/analyzer work must respect.
- **Item 2 — phenotype production validation: the only open item, a sequential chain.** Each step's
  numbers are unusable until the step before it lands, so the order below is load-bearing, not a
  checklist. History: measured twice on 2026-07-30 (full write-up
  `jb/docs/ImageNGP/phenotype-assignment-validation.md`) — rule fixes moved SPACINI29 from correct
  15 / wrong 38 to correct 33 / wrong 13 with no threshold change; the 13 survivors are all upstream
  ([[T-4990]] detector under-count, [[T-5000]] filename orientation, CLIP orientation argmax 74%).
  Then on 2026-07-31, `BypassPhenotypes` was removed (commit `416ae33`, [[T-5010]]) and CLIP thresholds
  were lowered the same day (commit `97326fe`: `hero-orientation` 0.60→0.33, `head-visible` 0.65→0.25,
  `body-visible` 0.60→0.10, `OrientationConfidence` 0.75→0.60) — **neither change has been re-measured**,
  and removing the bypass woke three defects that had been dormant underneath it. In order:
  1. Re-measure at the new thresholds and with the bypass removed — the immediate next step; the
     0.30-bar figures in the doc predict but do not confirm the new numbers. Also closes [[T-5010]]'s
     verification and unblocks a [[T-4970]] review verdict.
  2. Fix [[T-4955]] (derived edge features go stale on promotion — 42% of SPACINI29 is
     self-contradictory as a result) — tuning against a snapshot that contradicts itself that often is
     tuning noise.
  3. Fix the two other routing defects the bypass had been masking: `FinalOutputSize.RoutesToCenterAndStretch`
     omits the `SelectedPhenotype is null` half of `SelectTransformer` Step 1 (flagged in the archived
     T-4910 review); and `Tx_ProblemImageProcessor` derives output metadata from the unscaled
     original-resolution field (flagged in the archived T-4920 review) — Step 1 now sends every
     null-phenotype image there.
  4. Then the full bar: labeled set, confusion matrix, <5% misassignment across all 18 phenotypes, no
     systematic error on any one.
     - Commission one labeled set — [[T-4945]] needs the same asset. The 22-image CiGolden candidate
       set this pointed at is gone: CiGolden and JBComplete were merged into `test/datasets/CiMini/` on
       2026-08-06 (JBComplete's content replaced CiMini's old 14-image fixture wholesale; CiGolden's
       candidates were retired, not carried over). The phenotype-gap analysis they drove is preserved
       in `test/datasets/CiMini/README.md` §3 history section.
     - The labeled set already exists and was never scored:
       `test/datasets/CiMini/expected-phenotype.json` (99 rows). Scored for the first time on
       2026-08-05 (in-process evidence harness, shipped config).
     - Label coverage is sufficient: 17 of 18 phenotypes have at least one positive case; only
       `illustration-technical-drawing` has none (README explains why — its two candidate images are
       marketing composites built on photographs, not line drawings). Excluding the 16 rows marked
       `"Confidence": "low"` (README §4.3), all 17 still keep at least one high-confidence positive
       case — the low-confidence rows do not carry any phenotype on their own.
     - The confusion matrix is now computable, so the "commission a labeled set" blocker is gone —
       step 4 is unblocked, it is not passed.
     - Result at shipped config — nowhere near the bar:

       | | coverage | misassignment (of all rows) | precision (of assigned) |
       |---|---|---|---|
       | all 99 rows | 39.4% (39/99) | **30.3%** (30 wrong) | 23.1% (9/39) |
       | 83 rows, low-confidence excluded | 38.6% (32/83) | **28.9%** (24 wrong) | 25.0% (8/32) |
       | SPACINI29, 86 rows (for contrast) | 33.7% | 4.7% | 86.2% |

       The M11 bar is <5% misassignment; JBComplete is at 30%. The two numbers do not conflict:
       SPACINI29 is homogeneous on-model apparel where only two phenotypes ever fire and they fire
       correctly; JBComplete has bags, footwear, ghost shots and packshots, and there the rules
       collapse. The 5% figure was never a pass — it was the artefact of a dataset that only exercises
       2 of 18 phenotypes. Worst per-label recall: `front-packshot` 0/25, `diagonal-packshot` 0/8,
       `side-packshot` 0/7, `front-on-model-full-product` 0/5. Worst per-assignment precision:
       `closeup-image` 0/8, `front-packshot` 0/3, `back-packshot` 1/7.
     - Two causes account for nearly all of it, both already ticketed: [[T-5070]] (the
       `intersection-count = 0` gate makes 75% of images unable to reach any packshot phenotype, and
       `closeup-image` sinks what it rejects) and [[T-5080]] (`hero-orientation` reads UNKNOWN on 37%
       and never once produces SIDEON). Neither is a threshold; both must land before step 4 is
       re-measured.
     - The 16 low-confidence rows need the human pass README §4.3 asks for, but they are not what is
       holding the number down — excluding them moves misassignment by only 1.4 points.

  Status: open, step 1 (re-measure) not yet started.
